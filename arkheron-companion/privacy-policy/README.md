## Arkheron Companion

### Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 25-08-2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arkheron Companion handles information when you use the app. Arkheron Companion is an independent community reference app and is not operated by Bonfire Studios.

### Information the app stores

- Favorites and selected app preferences are stored locally on your device using platform preferences.
- Spires planner state and purchase history are used to calculate the planner view during the app session. Favorites persist locally between sessions.
- The app does not require an account, login, or registration.
- The app does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, or payment information.

### Network requests

When online, the app may check a public wiki manifest, refresh public reference data, and request public artwork from MediaWiki. Those services may receive normal technical request data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. The app does not send your local favorites or planner preferences to those services.

The bundled reference library remains available offline. Network refreshes can be disabled by going offline or by platform controls.

### Analytics and advertising

The app currently contains no advertising SDK, behavioural analytics, user profiling, or sale of personal data.

### Data deletion

You can delete local app data through your device settings or uninstall the app. Removing the app removes locally stored preferences. Public wiki providers handle their server logs under their own policies.

### Third parties and intellectual property

Arkheron, its game content, and related marks belong to Bonfire Studios. This independent companion does not claim ownership of that material. Public wiki and artwork providers may have separate terms and licences.

### Security and changes

Reasonable measures are used to avoid collecting unnecessary data. No internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy may change when the app’s data flows change.
