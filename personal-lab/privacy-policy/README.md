## Personal Lab Website

### Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 25-08-2026

This website is a static portfolio and notebook for software projects, AI writing, and experiments.

### Information collected

The current site does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, or payment information. It currently contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking pixels, or behavioural profiling.

The theme preference may be stored locally in your browser using `localStorage`.

If you contact the site by email, your email address and message will be handled by your email provider and by the site owner for the purpose of replying.

### Hosting and links

When hosted, the hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP address, browser information, and server logs under its own privacy policy. External links, including GitHub, LinkedIn, and email providers, have their own policies.

### Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if analytics, mailing lists, payments, accounts, or other data collection are added. Privacy questions can be sent to [pocean.development@gmail.com](mailto:pocean.development@gmail.com).

This document is a plain-language product policy and is not legal advice.
