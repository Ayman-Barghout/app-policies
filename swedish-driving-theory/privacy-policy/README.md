## Swedish Driving Theory / Körkortsteori

### Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 25-08-2026

Swedish Driving Theory is an offline-first study app for Swedish category B licence theory.

### Information stored

- Quiz progress, scores, and mock-exam history are stored locally on your device using platform preferences.
- The app does not require an account or login.
- The app does not intentionally collect names, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, or payment information.
- The app currently has no backend, behavioural analytics, advertising SDK, or user profiling.

### Network and third parties

The current study content and illustrations are bundled in the app and do not require a network connection. The app does not intentionally send quiz answers, progress, or personal information to a server.

If a future version adds accounts, analytics, advertising, payments, or remote content, this policy will be updated before that feature is enabled.

### Data deletion

Delete app data through device settings or uninstall the app. This removes local progress and exam history.

### Important content note

This is independent study support, not Trafikverket’s official test. Traffic rules, signs, and examination arrangements can change. Check current official Swedish authority information.
